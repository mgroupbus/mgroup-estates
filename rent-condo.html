FAQ

Questions people ask

Can I rent monthly or yearly?

Yes. We arrange monthly, 6-month and 12-month leases, subject to each owner's terms.

Can I see the condominium before I sign?

Yes. Every rental includes a viewing, in person or by video call, confirmed with you in advance.

How much is the deposit?

For leases of 6 months or more, usually 2 months' rent as a security deposit plus the first month in advance. Short stays vary by owner. We confirm the exact amount in writing before you sign, and the deposit is returned at the end of the lease after the unit check.

Are pets allowed?

It depends on the condominium's rules and the owner. Tell us about your pet when you book and we only show you units that accept pets.

What does Nest+ concierge include?

Every MGROUP Estates tenant can use Nest+ concierge: help with move-in, internet and utilities setup, cleaning and laundry, car or scooter rental, airport transfers and repairs during your lease.
