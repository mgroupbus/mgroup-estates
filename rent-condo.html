FAQ

Questions people ask

Which condos can I rent?

The Standard Residence Bangtao, The Aqua, The Space Choeng Thale, The Base Choeng Thale, Cassia, Sky Park and Canvas Sansiri, all in the Bang Tao and Cherng Talay area of Phuket.

How does renting work?

1. Request an appointment. 2. Our admin contacts you. 3. We confirm a viewing date. 4. You sign the lease.

How much is the rent?

It depends on the condo, unit size, view, season and lease length. Send your dates and budget and we reply with current units and rates.

Can I rent monthly or yearly?

Yes. We arrange monthly, 6-month and 12-month leases, subject to each owner's terms.

Can I see the condo before I sign?

Yes. Every rental includes a viewing, in person or by video call, confirmed with you in advance.

I own a condo. Can you rent it out for me?

Yes. Send your unit details. We prepare the listing, advertise it, screen tenants, arrange viewings and prepare the lease.

Which languages do you speak?

Thai, English and Russian.
