Privacy Policy

MGROUP Estates ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect the personal information you share with us through our website and property enquiries.

Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and any details you include in your message. We also collect anonymous analytics data about how visitors use our website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide property consultancy services
  • To share property listings and market updates relevant to your interest
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers (e.g., email delivery, CRM) strictly for the purpose of operating our business. Property partners may receive your enquiry only when you explicitly request introduction.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@mgroupestates.com.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure or loss. However, no online transmission is completely secure.

Cookie Policy

This policy explains how MGROUP Estates uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how visitors interact with content.

Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function properly (e.g., remembering your cookie consent).
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. These are anonymous.
  • Preference cookies — remember your language and display settings.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use services such as Google Fonts and embedded video providers that set their own cookies. These third parties have their own privacy policies.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies entirely or be notified before one is set.

Contact

For questions about our use of cookies, please email contact@mgroupestates.com.
